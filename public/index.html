        How it works

        1. The loan. cirBTC (bitcoin on Arc) is locked in Circle's Borrow Kit and USDC is borrowed against it. If bitcoin falls under the line, the bitcoin is sold off.
        2. The odds. Gemini's prediction market prices "BTC at $X or above on a date". Airbag turns those prices into the chance bitcoin dips under your line.
        3. The airbag. Spare USDC waits in a savings vault (Circle's Earn Kit) earning interest. When the odds pass your limit, the bot moves some of it into the loan. When things calm down, it moves it back.
